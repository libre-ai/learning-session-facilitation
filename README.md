# Libre AI Learning Session Facilitation

Prepare and follow a learning session with clear source material, participant roles and responsibilities. This proposed product is for trainers and facilitators who want to organize an activity and keep track of how the session unfolds.


## Intended uses

- Gather cited material to support a workshop or training session.
- Clarify what participants are expected to do and which tasks may be delegated.
- Follow the session's events and review what happened afterwards.
